08 / Authorship & Methodology

About REFLEX Research Laboratory

Independent Mechanism Research

REFLEX is an independent research environment for studying The Standard Reserve from two complementary angles: exit-feedback behavioral dynamics (Dynamics) and executable protocol specifications (SpecLab).

Disclaimer: REFLEX is not affiliated with The Standard Reserve. It is not an audit, financial advice, or an endorsement. Unpublished implementation details are marked rather than guessed.
