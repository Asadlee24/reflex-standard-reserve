03 / Mathematical Formulation

Published shape vs. research assumptions.

REFLEX separates the mechanics derived from publicly described documentation from the behavioral response layer modeled for research.

Published Mechanism Shape Trailing Window

Exit Pressure & Quadratic Resolution Fee

P_t = W_t / (D_t + W_t)
Fee_t = Floor + (Ceiling − Floor) × clamp(P_t / Saturation, 0, 1)²

Trailing exit volume over trailing exits plus currently held value determines exit pressure. The resolution fee follows a quadratic interpolation bounded between the fee floor and ceiling.

REFLEX Research Assumption Behavioral Layer

Participant Response & Contagion Feedback

Score_t = Baseline + (c × Exit_{t-1}) − (d × Fee_t)
Pr(Exit_t) = logistic(β × Score_t)

Modeled participants experience contagion from recent exit rates while being deterred by elevated resolution fees. This sensitivity model evaluates system stability under varying assumptions.
